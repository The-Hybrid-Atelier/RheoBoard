# Sensing Setup

This is the 3D-printed GL45 cap and the tube for that cap. It is used with a GL45 bottle. The bottle is not a printed part. Print notes are on the [Parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-3D-print) wiki page.

- `caps/` — `cap_easier_thread_fit.stl` and `cap_non.stl`.
- `tubes/` — `tube_100mm.stl`, `tube_150mm.stl`, and `tube_200mm.stl`, the matching `*_barbed.stl` files, and `hose_connector_3mm_tubing.stl`.
- `images/` — `gl45-cap-and-tube.jpg` is one picture of the printed cap and tube on a GL45 bottle. The side view is on the left and the top view is on the right.
- `references/` — [`DURAN_Caps_and_Connection_Systems.pdf`](references/DURAN_Caps_and_Connection_Systems.pdf) is the DURAN caps and connection systems catalog, kept as a reference for the GL45 cap. It stays under the publisher's terms.
- `oldver/` — earlier Autodesk Inventor STEP files, `nap.stp` and `lap.stp`. `lap.stp` includes the `nap` part and `part7ver2`. They are not the current print files.
