# Sensing Setup

This is the 3D-printed GL45 cap and the tube for that cap. It is used with a GL45 bottle. The bottle is not a printed part. Print notes are on the [Parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-3D-print) wiki page.

`gl45-cap-and-tube.jpg` is one picture of the printed cap and tube on a GL45 bottle. The side view is on the left and the top view is on the right.

`cap_easier_thread_fit.stl` and `cap_non.stl` are caps. `tube_100mm.stl`, `tube_150mm.stl`, and `tube_200mm.stl` are tubes, and the matching `*_barbed.stl` files are the barbed versions. `hose_connector_3mm_tubing.stl` is a hose connector for 3 mm tubing.
