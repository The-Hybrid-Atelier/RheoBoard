# Parts to 3D print

Print files are on the [Parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-3D-print) wiki page. That page opens with the annotated photo of the RheoBoardOTS panel, [`panel-annotated.pdf`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/concept/panel-annotated.pdf), and the lid-off drawing, [`assembly-open.jpg`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/concept/assembly-open.jpg).

[`RheoBoardOTS_CAD`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/) is the CAD design for RheoBoardOTS: layout drawings, plates, the other parts to print, and a tray for each electronics board on this build. [`Sensing Setup`](../Sensing%20Setup/) is the 3D-printed GL45 cap and tube. The bottle is not a printed part. RheoPipette print files are on the [Portable parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Portable-parts-to-3D-print) page.

The plate for this build is [`part_01_large_two_L298N.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/part_01_large_two_L298N.stl), 218 × 208 × 3 mm. Its drawing is [`plate_two_L298N_top.png`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/images/plate_two_L298N_top.png). [`part_01_large_one_L298N.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/part_01_large_one_L298N.stl) stays in the CAD folder. It is not the current layout. The other parts are [`pump_holder1.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/pump_holder1.stl) and [`valve_bracket.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/valve_bracket.stl). [`part_03_valve_bracket_1.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/part_03_valve_bracket_1.stl) is the same mesh as the valve bracket.

Print [`ESP32_ThingPlus_tray.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/ESP32_ThingPlus_tray.stl), [`L298N_tray.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/L298N_tray.stl) twice, [`MicroPressure_tray.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/MicroPressure_tray.stl), and [`PCA9685_ServoDriver_tray.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/PCA9685_ServoDriver_tray.stl). The pictures of each board in its tray are in [`images`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/images/). There is no button-tray file. The Qwiic button sits in the printed holder shown in [`rheoboardots-built-panel.jpg`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/images/rheoboardots-built-panel.jpg).

Sensing setup files are in [`Sensing Setup`](../Sensing%20Setup/): caps in `caps/`, tubes in `tubes/`, the photo in [`images/gl45-cap-and-tube.jpg`](../Sensing%20Setup/images/gl45-cap-and-tube.jpg), and the DURAN catalog in [`references/DURAN_Caps_and_Connection_Systems.pdf`](../Sensing%20Setup/references/DURAN_Caps_and_Connection_Systems.pdf). The printed cap and tube are used with a GL45 bottle. The side view is on the left and the top view is on the right. The tube passes through the cap and hangs in the bottle. The top of the cap has one threaded hole and the tube fitting. The catalog stays under the publisher's terms. Earlier Inventor STEP files are in [`oldver`](../Sensing%20Setup/oldver): [`nap.stp`](../Sensing%20Setup/oldver/nap.stp) and [`lap.stp`](../Sensing%20Setup/oldver/lap.stp). `lap.stp` includes the `nap` part and `part7ver2`. They are not the current print files. Current print files named on the wiki are [`cap_easier_thread_fit.stl`](../Sensing%20Setup/caps/cap_easier_thread_fit.stl), [`tube_100mm.stl`](../Sensing%20Setup/tubes/tube_100mm.stl), and [`hose_connector_3mm_tubing.stl`](../Sensing%20Setup/tubes/hose_connector_3mm_tubing.stl).
