# Parts to 3D print

Print files are on the [Parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-3D-print) wiki page. [`RheoBoardOTS_CAD`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/) is the CAD design for RheoBoardOTS: a layout drawing, a plate to print, the other parts to print, and a tray for each electronics board. [`Sensing Setup`](../Sensing%20Setup/) is the 3D-printed GL45 cap and tube. The bottle is not a printed part. Concept visuals are in `BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/concept/`. RheoBoardPipette print files are on the [Portable parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Portable-parts-to-3D-print) page.

The layout drawing is [`plate_one_L298N_top.png`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/images/plate_one_L298N_top.png). It is the one-L298N layout, 190 × 215 × 3 mm, with 42 holes. PWM / PCA9685 is on the left and the ESP32 is on the right. The drawing says physical fit is unverified.

The plate to print is [`part_01_large_one_L298N.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/part_01_large_one_L298N.stl), 190 × 215 × 3 mm. The other parts are [`pump_holder1.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/pump_holder1.stl) and [`valve_bracket.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/valve_bracket.stl). [`part_03_valve_bracket_1.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/part_03_valve_bracket_1.stl) is the same mesh as the valve bracket.

Trays: [`ESP32_ThingPlus_tray.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/ESP32_ThingPlus_tray.stl), [`L298N_tray.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/L298N_tray.stl), [`MicroPressure_tray.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/MicroPressure_tray.stl), and [`PCA9685_ServoDriver_tray.stl`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/PCA9685_ServoDriver_tray.stl). The pictures of each board in its tray are in [`images`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/images/).

Sensing setup files: [`cap_easier_thread_fit.stl`](../Sensing%20Setup/cap_easier_thread_fit.stl), [`tube_100mm.stl`](../Sensing%20Setup/tube_100mm.stl), and [`hose_connector_3mm_tubing.stl`](../Sensing%20Setup/hose_connector_3mm_tubing.stl).
