# Images

Photos and figures for the public pages. Component photos support the [Parts to buy](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-buy) wiki page, and the source notes for these files are kept with the wiki images. Part folders are `air-pump-4699/`, `air-valve-4663/`, `attiny1616-seesaw/`, `battery-prt-26059/`, `esp32-thing-plus/`, `l298n/`, `power-adapter-alt-1202/`, `qwiic-button/`, `qwiic-micropressure/`, `silicone-tube-4661/`, `qwiic-cable/`, `micro-usb-cable/`, `ftllb220-6005/`, `gl45-bottle/`, and `teaser/`. `panel/` and `portable/` hold the panel and portable figures (`rheopipette.jpg`, `thing_plus_tray.png`, `micropressure_tray.png`, and `battery_tray.png`), and `home-versions.png` is the home figure. The third panel is an October 25, 2026 v1.05 board snapshot, before the valve-rail and sensor-reset changes; current drawings are indexed in the [PCB README](../../RheoBoard_PCB/README.md). The fourth panel of that figure is the current RheoPipette photo. `hybrid-atelier-logo.png` is The Hybrid Atelier mark after the author line on the repository README. `rep-sensing-method.jpg` is the sensing-method diagram, `rep-how-a-rep-looks.jpg` is the chart on How a REP looks, `rep-pressure-trace.jpg` is an earlier pressure trace, `rep-bench.jpg` is the bench photo, `rep-sensing-and-bench.jpg` is an older side-by-side pair; the wiki shows the diagram and the bench photo as two pictures, and `rep-phase-schematic.jpg` is the phase schematic. The certification marks in this folder are `oshwa-certification-mark-US002865-wide.svg`, `oshwa-certification-mark-US002865-stacked.svg`, and `oshwa-license-facts.svg`. Vendor photos stay under their owners' terms.
