# Images

Photos and figures for the public pages. Component photos support the [Parts to buy](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-buy) wiki page, and the source notes for these files are kept with the wiki images. Part folders are `air-pump-4699/`, `air-valve-4663/`, `attiny1616-seesaw/`, `battery-prt-26059/`, `esp32-thing-plus/`, `l298n/`, `power-adapter-alt-1202/`, `qwiic-button/`, `qwiic-micropressure/`, `silicone-tube-4661/`, and `teaser/`. `panel/` and `portable/` hold the panel and portable figures, and `home-versions.png` is the home figure. `rep-sensing-method.jpg` is the sensing-method diagram, `rep-pressure-trace.jpg` is the pressure trace over the REP window, with Δpr at the retraction low point, Δpe at the extrusion high point, decay τ on the settling side, and the firmware phase lengths noted as schematic, not measured. `rep-bench.jpg` is the bench photo with the probe, actuator, and sensor. `rep-sensing-and-bench.jpg` is those two pictures cropped and placed side by side, the sensing-method diagram on the left and the bench photo on the right. `rep-phase-schematic.jpg` is the schematic those labels were taken from. It is not on the wiki page. The certification marks in this folder are `oshwa-certification-mark-US002865-wide.svg`, `oshwa-certification-mark-US002865-stacked.svg`, and `oshwa-license-facts.svg`. Vendor photos stay under their owners' terms.
