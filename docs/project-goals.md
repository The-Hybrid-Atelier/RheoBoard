# Project goals

From [`README.md`](../README.md):

RheoBoard is open hardware for pneumatic retraction-extrusion measurements. On command, it runs a REP (retraction-extrusion pulse) and streams the pressure trace.

From `okh-RheoBoard.yml` `intended-use`:

Bench research and teaching instrument for characterizing the pneumatic pressure response of soft/viscous samples. Built and operated by makers, students, and researchers comfortable with the Arduino IDE and reading wiring diagrams. Soldering is only needed when headers or actuator leads are not already fitted. Not a medical or safety-critical device.
