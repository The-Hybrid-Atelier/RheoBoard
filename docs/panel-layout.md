# Panel layout

The current layout is the one-L298N drawing [`plate_one_L298N_top.png`](../BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/images/plate_one_L298N_top.png). It is 190 × 215 × 3 mm, with 42 holes. PWM / PCA9685 is on the left and the ESP32 is on the right. The drawing says physical fit is unverified. Placement on the built panel is [Step 01 of the assembly instructions](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Assembly-instructions#step-01-assemble-the-platform).

The annotated photo of the RheoBoardOTS panel is `panel-annotated.pdf` in `BuildYourOwn/RheoBoardOTS/cad/concept/`. The lid-off drawing is `assembly-open.jpg` in that folder. Those two pictures open the [Parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-3D-print) wiki page.
