# Images

Project and component photos for RheoBoardOTS - DIY. These photos support the purchased parts on the [Parts to buy](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-buy) wiki page.

`teaser.jpg` is an earlier assembled bench prototype. `rheoboardots-built-panel.jpg` is the current built panel. The component photos are `esp32-thing-plus.jpg`, `qwiic-micropressure.jpg`, `qwiic-button.jpg`, `adafruit-4699-air-pump.jpg`, `adafruit-4663-air-valve.jpg`, `power-adapter-alt-1202.jpg`, and `l298n-motor-driver.jpg`. Vendor photos stay under their owners' terms and are here so a builder can recognize the part. `adafruit-815-top.jpg` shows product 815 before its headers are fitted.

The RheoBoardOTS CAD pictures are here as well. `plate_two_L298N_top.png` is the drawing for this build. `plate_one_L298N_top.png` is the one-L298N drawing and is not the current layout. `ESP32_ThingPlus_tray_with_module.png`, `L298N_tray_with_module.png`, `MicroPressure_tray_with_module.png`, and `PCA9685_ServoDriver_tray_with_module.png` show each board in its tray. The print files stay in [`RheoBoardOTS_CAD`](../cad/RheoBoardOTS_CAD/).

<img src="rheoboardots-labeled-overview.png?v=b43b52849bda" width="480" alt="Labeled RheoBoardOTS panel overview">

`rheoboardots-labeled-overview.png` is the illustrated component overview, rendered from the built-panel reference with wiring and tubing hidden for clarity. Use the [assembly instructions](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Assembly-instructions) for connections.

The tubing figure uses these Adafruit port close-ups: [pump inlet and outlet](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/970x728/4699-08.jpg) (`adafruit-4699-ports.jpg`), [valve plastic end and common](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/970x728/4663-06.jpg) (`adafruit-4663-plastic-ports.jpg`), and [valve metal end](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/970x728/4663-07.jpg) (`adafruit-4663-metal-port.jpg`). They are embedded unchanged with connection markers in the [tube-wiring figure](../hardware/tube-wiring/README.md).

Assembly Steps 03 and 07 also use the [Adafruit 815 top photo](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/970x728/815-10.jpg), plus `l298n-module.jpg` and `l298n-terminal-reference.jpg`, extracted from pages 1 and 3 of the [Handson Technology L298N guide](https://handsontec.com/dataspecs/module/L298N%20Motor%20Driver.pdf). The latter is displayed through a board-only viewport in the figure; its existing supplier annotations remain. `micro-usb-cable.jpg` reuses the cable photo already published with the wiki parts list. These are component-identification photos; the driver images show the supplied jumper caps, with Step 03 marking which to remove.
