# RheoBoardOTS CAD

This is the CAD design for RheoBoardOTS. Print notes and previews are on the [Parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-3D-print) wiki page.

Layout drawings are in [`images`](../../images/): `plate_one_L298N_top.png` (one L298N) and `plate_two_L298N_top.png` (two L298N modules). The drawings say physical fit is unverified.

Plates to print: `part_01_large_one_L298N.stl` is the one-L298N plate, 190 × 214 × 3 mm. `part_01_large_two_L298N.stl` is the two-L298N plate, 218 × 208 × 3 mm.

Other parts to print: `pump_holder1.stl` and `pump_holder2.stl` are pump holders. `valve_bracket.stl` is the valve bracket, and `part_03_valve_bracket_1.stl` is the same mesh.

Trays, one for each electronics board: `ESP32_ThingPlus_tray.stl`, `L298N_tray.stl`, `MicroPressure_tray.stl`, and `PCA9685_ServoDriver_tray.stl`. The pictures of each board in its tray are in [`images`](../../images/): `ESP32_ThingPlus_tray_with_module.png`, `L298N_tray_with_module.png`, `MicroPressure_tray_with_module.png`, and `PCA9685_ServoDriver_tray_with_module.png`.
