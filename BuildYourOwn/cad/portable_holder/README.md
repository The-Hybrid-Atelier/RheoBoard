# Portable holder

Three shallow trays for the RheoBoardPipette - Portable holder. Print notes are on the [Portable parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Portable-parts-to-3D-print) wiki page.

`thing_plus_tray.stp` is the tray for the SparkFun ESP32 Thing Plus (micro-USB), about 40 × 57.8 × 5 mm. `micropressure_tray.stp` is the tray for the SparkFun Qwiic MicroPressure sensor, about 30 × 30 × 5 mm. `battery_tray.stp` is the tray for the SparkFun Lithium Ion Battery 1500 mAh (PRT-26059), about 30 × 61.6 × 5 mm, with a notch in one long side. Each tray has a 1 mm floor, and the rim brings the part to 5 mm tall.
