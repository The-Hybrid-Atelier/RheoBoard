# Portable holder

Three shallow trays for the RheoBoardPipette - Portable holder. Print notes are on the [Portable parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Portable-parts-to-3D-print) wiki page.

`rectangular_tray.stp` is a rectangular tray, about 40 × 57.8 × 5 mm. `notched_tray.stp` is a rectangular tray, about 30 × 61.6 × 5 mm, with a notch in one long side. `square_tray.stp` is a square tray, about 30 × 30 × 5 mm. Each tray has a 1 mm floor, and the rim brings the part to 5 mm tall.
