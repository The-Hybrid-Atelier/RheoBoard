# Panel print files

These STEP files are the pieces of the flat panel assembly. Print notes for the panel, pump holder, and valve bracket are on the [Parts to 3D print](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Parts-to-3D-print) wiki page.

`base_panel.STEP` is the flat panel. `pump_holder.STEP` holds one air pump. `valve_bracket.STEP` is the small bracket for the valve. `enclosure_box.STEP` is the box, and `enclosure_lid.STEP` is its lid.
