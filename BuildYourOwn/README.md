# RheoBoardOTS - DIY

This folder is RheoBoardOTS - DIY: a flat 3D-printed panel, two air pumps, one valve, an ESP32 with BLE, a Qwiic MicroPressure sensor, and a REP (retraction-extrusion pulse).

The steps are on the [Assembly instructions](https://github.com/The-Hybrid-Atelier/RheoBoard/wiki/Assembly-instructions) wiki page.

Design files are in [`cad/`](cad/), the parts list and wiring notes are in [`hardware/`](hardware/), and photos are in [`images/`](images/).
